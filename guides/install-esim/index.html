How to install a travel eSIM on iPhone and Android

Step-by-step: check your phone supports eSIM, install the plan before you fly, and switch it on when you land.

1. Check that your phone supports eSIM

Most iPhones from the XS/XR (2018) onwards and most recent Samsung Galaxy and Google Pixel phones support eSIM. The phone must also be unlocked (not tied to one carrier). A quick test: dial *#06#. If you see an EID number, your phone has eSIM.

2. Install it at home, before you fly

After you buy a plan you get a QR code or an install button. Install it on Wi-Fi a day or two before the trip. Most plans only start counting days once they connect at your destination; check the plan's details to be sure.

On iPhone

  • Settings, then Cellular (called Mobile Service or Mobile Data in some countries)
  • Tap Add eSIM, then Use QR Code, and scan the code (or tap the provider's install link on the phone itself)
  • Give it a label like "Travel" and keep your main line for calls and messages

On Samsung Galaxy

  • Settings, then Connections, then SIM manager
  • Tap Add eSIM and scan the QR code

On Google Pixel

  • Settings, then Network & internet, then SIMs
  • Tap Add SIM (or Download a SIM instead) and scan the QR code

3. When you land

  • Turn the travel eSIM on and set it as the line for mobile data
  • Turn on data roaming for the travel eSIM (this is normal for travel eSIMs and does not cost extra)
  • Turn off data roaming on your home line, so your own carrier does not charge you
  • Keep your home line on for calls and SMS if you need codes from your bank

If it doesn't connect

  • Check that data roaming is on for the eSIM line
  • Restart the phone and wait a minute
  • Pick a network by hand in the network settings
  • Contact the provider's support with your order number
