One eSIM for several countries: how to plan a multi-country trip

Regional plans, one plan per country, or a mix: how to get data for a trip through several countries without overpaying.

Two ways to cover several countries

  • A regional plan (for example Europe or Asia) covers many countries on one eSIM, so you install once and keep going
  • A separate plan per country can be cheaper when you spend most of your time in one place

When a regional plan wins

If you cross borders often, or spend just a few days in each country, one regional plan is usually simpler and often cheaper overall. Check that every country on your route is actually included; regional plans differ a lot in coverage.

When one plan per country wins

If most of your trip is in one country, a local plan there plus a small plan for the side trip can cost less. Several eSIMs can sit on the same phone; you just pick which one to use for data.
