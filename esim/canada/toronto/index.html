Good to know

Do I need a separate eSIM for Toronto?

No. Any Canada eSIM works in Toronto and the rest of Canada, so compare plans for the whole country.

How much is an eSIM for 5 days in Toronto?

From $1.03 with at least 1 GB (Stellar eSIM), or $4.90 for about 1 GB a day (ASIMGO). Prices are updated daily.

What is the cheapest eSIM for Toronto?

Today the cheapest plan with at least 1 GB for 7 days is Stellar eSIM at $1.03 (1 GB). Prices change daily; this page is refreshed every day.

How much data do I need for 7 days in Toronto?

For maps, messages and some browsing, about 0.5 GB a day is enough, so around 4 GB for 7 days. If you stream video or share your connection with a laptop, take 10 GB or an unlimited plan.

Does my phone support eSIM?

Dial *#06#. If you see a number called EID, your phone has eSIM. Most iPhones since the XS, Google Pixel 3 and newer, and Samsung Galaxy S20 and newer support it. The phone must be unlocked.

When should I install it?

Install the eSIM at home over Wi-Fi a day or two before you fly, and switch mobile data to it when you land. Most plans only start counting days when they first connect in Toronto.

Will I keep my own number?

Yes. Your regular SIM stays in the phone, so WhatsApp and your number keep working. The travel eSIM is for data; calls and texts in these plans go through apps like WhatsApp.
