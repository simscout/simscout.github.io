Good to know

What plug do I need in Macao?

Macao uses plug type D, G, M at 220 V. Most phone chargers work on any voltage, so you usually need only a plug adapter.

What is the emergency number in Macao?

The emergency number in Macao is 999.

What is the cheapest eSIM for Macao?

Today the cheapest plan with at least 1 GB for 7 days is Stellar eSIM at $0.53 (1 GB). Prices change daily; this page is refreshed every day.

How much data do I need for 7 days in Macao?

For maps, messages and some browsing, about 0.5 GB a day is enough, so around 4 GB for 7 days. If you stream video or share your connection with a laptop, take 10 GB or an unlimited plan.

Does my phone support eSIM?

Dial *#06#. If you see a number called EID, your phone has eSIM. Most iPhones since the XS, Google Pixel 3 and newer, and Samsung Galaxy S20 and newer support it. The phone must be unlocked.

When should I install it?

Install the eSIM at home over Wi-Fi a day or two before you fly, and switch mobile data to it when you land. Most plans only start counting days when they first connect in Macao.

Will I keep my own number?

Yes. Your regular SIM stays in the phone, so WhatsApp and your number keep working. The travel eSIM is for data; calls and texts in these plans go through apps like WhatsApp.
