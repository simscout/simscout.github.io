Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026 (data-used tracker and map image sharing added)

SimScout ("we") is a free app and website that compares travel eSIM plans. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the SimScout Android app or simscout.github.io.

What we collect

SimScout has no sign-up and no account. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. The Android app collects anonymous usage data, described under "Analytics" below.

What stays on your device

Your chosen country, trip dates, filters, saved plans, language and currency, and your list of eSIMs you used with your ratings, are stored only on your own device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser data deletes them.

Links to providers

When you tap a plan, the provider's own website opens. Some of these links are affiliate links that tell the provider you came from SimScout, so we may earn a commission if you buy. The provider's site has its own privacy policy, and anything you enter there (such as payment details) goes to the provider, not to us.

Analytics

To understand which screens and features are used and where people get stuck, the Android app uses Microsoft Clarity. Clarity records anonymous usage data: screens viewed, taps and scrolls (including session replays and heatmaps), which provider was opened, your app settings such as language and currency, and technical details like device model, operating system and approximate country. It does not record your name or contact details, and SimScout has no text fields that ask for personal information. Microsoft processes this data under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

You can turn analytics off at any time in the app under More, Privacy. The website version does not use analytics.

Advertising

SimScout does not show ads and does not use advertising trackers.

Permissions

The Android app uses the internet to refresh prices. If you turn on a trip reminder, it asks permission to show a notification the evening before your trip, and optionally on the evening you get home; reminders are stored only on your phone. If you choose to see how much of your travel eSIM's data you have used, Android asks you to allow “Usage access”; SimScout then reads only the total amount of mobile data the phone used since your trip started. This number is shown on your phone and is never sent anywhere. You can turn Usage access off at any time in Android settings. When you share your travel map, the app creates the picture on your phone and passes it only to the app you choose. It does not use your contacts, camera, microphone, files or location.

Children

SimScout is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
